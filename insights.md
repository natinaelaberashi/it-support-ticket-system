# Example Findings

Using the synthetic sample:

- 15 transactions were analysed.
- 10 were approved and 5 declined, giving a 66.67% approval rate in this small sample.
- Declines are split between DO_NOT_HONOR and INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS.
- Mobile transactions show different processing and decline behaviour from Web transactions.
- A production workflow would segment results by PSP, payment method, country, currency, 3DS outcome and time window before drawing operational conclusions.

The purpose is to demonstrate the analysis workflow, not to represent real company performance.
